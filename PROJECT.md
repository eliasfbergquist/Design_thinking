# Dinder

A phone-first prototype for a flat that eats together. Roommates swipe meals, the week fills in from what everyone can actually eat, and cooking and dishes rotate with a quiet lean toward fairness.

This file is the project brain. Update it when the product, the rules, or the build change.

## Status

- Step 1 is in the browser: swipe, a shared week, cook mode, allergies, and a rota.
- The app is called Dinder. The header mark is a flame sitting in a bowl.
- Stack: Vite + React, data in `localStorage`. No backend.
- Demo flat: Jonas, Maja, Noah, Linnea, Sam. One hundred dinners, twenty of them photographed and the rest drawn.
- Run `npm run dev` and open the URL Vite prints. On a laptop it sits in a phone frame. On a narrow screen it is full bleed. Same Wi-Fi: use the Network URL on a phone.

## Why this shape

The useful thing to prototype is the daily loop, not accounts or scale.

1. Someone has the phone and swipes a few dinners.
2. The week proposes meals the people who are actually home can eat.
3. Cook and dishes fall to whoever is free, leaning toward people who have done it less, without turning that into a score.
4. Tonight opens into a cook view you can leave on the counter.

Web is only the delivery wrapper. Layout, type, and tap targets are for a phone held in one hand.

## Non-goals

- Accounts, sync, or a real backend.
- Nutrition math or grocery checkout.
- Hardening, tests-as-a-product, or scale.
- Treating two similar dinners as one dish with inherited votes. Each recipe is its own card.

Adaptability matters more than completeness. Rules live in `src/logic.js` and are described here so they can be changed without a rewrite.

## Product rules

### Eating together

The default is that everyone is in for dinner. A person can sit out a day. The whole flat can mark a day as eating out (no meal, no duties, rota does not move).

### Swipes

Swipes belong to whoever is marked as holding the phone.

- **Yes** — I'd eat this.
- **Love** — cook it soon. Weighs more than yes.
- **Pass** — don't plan this for me while I'm eating.

A pass is a veto for *automatic* planning only. The flat can still pick that meal by hand.

The deck order is fixed. Voting removes a card and the one behind it is the next one. It does not reshuffle.

### Allergies, diet, and swaps

Hard conflicts are never auto-planned:

- An allergen on a required ingredient.
- Meat or fish for a vegetarian who is eating that night.

**Table swaps** are optional ingredients with an allergen (cheddar on the tacos, parmesan on the tomato pasta). The meal stays eligible. The cook view names who needs the swap.

Allergies and diet are editable. Unlocked future dinners are resuggested when they change. Dinners someone explicitly chose stay put, with a warning if they no longer fit.

### Planning

The next few weeks start suggested, so there is something to react to. Suggested meals stay unlocked.

- **Another idea** or picking from the list locks that dinner.
- **Resuggest** draws again for every unlocked night. It will not keep the meal that was just there if anything else fits. Loved and yes votes are more likely, unseen meals can still surface, and a dinner already on this week is pushed down.
- The first fill of the week is stable, so reloading does not shuffle it. Resuggest is the shuffle.
- If nothing is safe, the day stays empty and says so.

### Duties

Cooking and washing are separate. The suggestion is a weighted draw among people who are eating and free for that job.

- Someone who has done less of that job lately is more likely to be picked. Someone ahead can still get it.
- Eating out, sitting out, or "can't cook / can't wash" excuses that night. It does not create a debt.
- There is no scoreboard. The counts stay in the assigner and are not shown.
- **Shuffle duties** draws again. A cook or dishwasher set by hand sticks until then.
- Solo dinners: they can cook for themselves, but washing your own plate is not a turn.

### Demo flat

- Wednesday: Linnea is out, so a meat dinner can win.
- Thursday: Noah can't cook.
- Friday: eating out at 19:00. Rota skips the day.

The actual dishes move when you resuggest. History is generated by the same assigner as the live app.

## Screens

- **Swipe** — deck of meals this person has not voted on. The card behind is the next card.
- **Plan** — tonight, then the week. Two weeks either way. Resuggest and Shuffle duties sit above the days.
- **Day** — who's in, schedule blocks, meal, duties, note. No fairness table.
- **Recipes** — book, search, "everyone can eat", grouped by shelf.
- **Cook** — ingredients with checks and a serving scaler, then one large step at a time. Screen wake lock when the browser allows it.
- **House** — allergies, diet, add a person, export/import, reset. No scoreboard.
- **Add recipe** — a new dish, not a variation of an old one.

There is no login. **Who's holding the phone** is the whole account model. Export / import JSON is how a second phone would pick up the flat.

## Data

`localStorage` key `dinder.v1`, wiped when `SEED_VERSION` changes.

- `roommates[]` — diet, allergies, color, order
- `recipes[]` — ingredients, steps, hard `allergens`, optional ingredient allergens
- `votes[personId][recipeId]` — `yes` | `love` | `no`
- `plans[YYYY-MM-DD]` — status, note, recipe, locks, cook, dish, per-person flags

The first twenty photos live in `public/food`. The other eighty are drawn in the app from the recipe's color and shelf, so the deck stays visual without eighty more downloads.

## Build

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the URL Vite prints. On a laptop the app sits in a phone-width column. On a narrow screen it is full bleed.

```bash
npm run shots
```

Writes viewport screenshots to `shots/` for UI passes. It uses the installed Microsoft Edge.

## Visual pass

- Phone frame on wide screens, full-bleed under 540px.
- Header is the flame-in-a-bowl mark plus the word Dinder.
- Cook view hides the tab bar. Step type is a serif around 28px. Next sits in the thumb zone.
- Swipe is drag plus Pass / Love / Yes. The card underneath is the one that lands.
- Allergen chips turn red only when someone in the flat actually has that allergy.
- Drawn dinners use a plate, taco, bowl, or pan in the recipe's color. Photographed ones keep their photos.

## Open questions

- How strong should the lean be when someone is well ahead on cooking?
